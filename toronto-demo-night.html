Toronto Demo Night

Entrepreneurs First · Inaugural Investment Fellows batch

As part of the inaugural Investment Fellows batch at Entrepreneurs First (startup incubator, $16B+ portfolio value), I ran Toronto Demo Night, a celebration of incredible Canadian builders. In the couple weeks before Demo Night, it grew to hundreds of RSVPs and 50,000+ views on the media I produced. Hosted at New Stadium in Toronto, everything was done on a <$1,000 budget.

The best review of the night wasn't ours. Someone snuck into Demo Night — then went home and wrote a 1,942-word blog post covering every single demo.
